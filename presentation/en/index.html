Tomi: learn the multiplication tables at your own pace

Tomi helps your child learn the multiplication tables in short sessions. The learning engine spots what they already know and brings back, at the right time, what is still shaky.

Android app in its testing phase, not yet available to everyone on Google Play.

What is Tomi for?

Tomi is an Android app for practising the multiplication tables, from 1 to 10, with an adult in charge. Sessions are short. The app remembers what the child gets right and what is still shaky, and brings it back at the right time. The parent follows progress in a space reserved for them.

Exercise modes

  • In orderComplete the table from start to finish.
  • BackwardsWe start at 10 and count down.
  • Mixed upThe multiplications come in a mixed-up order.
  • BurstOne multiplication at a time: answer fast, you have 90 seconds.

How to use it

  1. Install Tomi and read the information on the first “Before you start” screen. Tick the box to agree that your child's answers are stored on this phone: without this agreement, the app cannot save their progress.
  2. Protect the parent space: choose a question only you know the answer to. It will be asked whenever someone taps the Parent button.
  3. Add your children: first name, age, avatar and colour. The first name, exact age and avatar stay on the phone.
  4. The child plays: they tap their avatar on the “Who is playing?” screen, pick a table, then an exercise mode. The app announces the mode before starting.
  5. You follow progress in the parent space, table by table, for each child.
  6. If you wish, you can export data to help improve the app: you see a preview, tick the children concerned, then send the file yourself with the messaging app of your choice. It is optional.
  7. You can erase everything at any time from the parent space.

Your data

The app has no Internet access: it cannot send anything by itself. There is no account and no advertising. Export is optional; exported data is pseudonymised (a code replaces the first name; neither the first name, the exact age nor the avatar is sent).
